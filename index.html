What Victory offers

Long-term wrestler development

We focus on fundamentals that keep working as athletes grow — not short-term moves that only work right now. Our goal is to help wrestlers build consistency, confidence, and skills that carry into high school and beyond.

Private coaching

Weekly private sessions

Private coaching sessions will be available weekly at $40 per wrestler, per session. Groups will be built around similar size and skill level, with a goal of 2–4 kids per session and a cap of 6 wrestlers to keep sessions productive.

  • First-come, first-served registration
  • Goal group size of 2–4 kids per session
  • Sessions capped at 6 wrestlers
  • At least one coach and one high school wrestler assisting

FAQ

Parent questions

Where does Victory Wrestling practice?

Victory Wrestling practices at Liberty High School in Liberty, Missouri.

Is regular season registration open?

Not yet. Registration is currently open for Fall Pre-Season through TeamSnap. Regular season registration will be added when available.

What does my wrestler need before participating?

Wrestlers need an active USA Wrestling card. Cards can be purchased through USA Wrestling at www.themat.com.

How much does Pre-Season cost?

Victory Pre-Season is $150 and runs September through October.

How do I pay for a drop-in session?

Drop-in session payments can be sent through Venmo to @VictoryWrestling.

Who should younger or newer wrestlers start with?

Matletics is the starting point for younger and newer wrestlers ages 5–8.

Who do I contact with questions?

Email info@victorywrestling.org.
